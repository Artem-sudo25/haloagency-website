## Product Overview
**One-liner:**
HaloAgency helps businesses in Czechia turn their website, ads, tracking, and automation into one connected lead and sales system.

**What it does:**
HaloAgency designs and builds landing pages, business websites, and e-commerce projects; runs Google Ads and Meta Ads; sets up tracking and analytics; and automates lead handling and operational workflows. The offer is not "one isolated service," but a connected commercial system where the site, traffic, data, and follow-up actually work together.

**Product category:**
Marketing agency, web and performance agency, lead generation agency, performance marketing + web development partner.

**Product type:**
Service business / agency.

**Business model:**
Project setup fees plus ongoing monthly work where relevant. The site also uses package-style pricing as a scope anchor, not as a full replacement for custom scoping.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:**
Small and medium-sized businesses in Czechia, especially service businesses, local businesses, e-commerce projects, hospitality/tourism businesses, and owner-led companies that need a clearer lead/sales system.

**Decision-makers:**
Founders, owners, directors, and practical marketing leads who care about leads, sales, and operational clarity more than agency theatre.

**Primary use case:**
The business needs more qualified leads or sales, but the current website, ads, analytics, and follow-up do not work as one system.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Get more qualified inquiries or sales from a clearer website and offer.
- Make paid ads measurable and worth scaling.
- Stop losing leads because the data, forms, or follow-up process are weak.

**Use cases:**
- Launch a new landing page or business site before or alongside advertising.
- Improve existing Google Ads or Meta Ads performance with better pages and tracking.
- Set up HaloTrack / server-side tracking and connect forms to a real lead flow.
- Add automation for lead handling, booking, reminders, CRM, and ops.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:**
The business has pieces of marketing activity, but not a connected system. The site, ads, analytics, and follow-up are misaligned.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- A design-only website does not solve conversion or lead flow problems.
- Ads-only management fails when the page, offer, or tracking is weak.
- Tracking-only work is not enough if the website and campaign logic are broken.
- Cheap freelancers often deliver a disconnected asset, not a working commercial system.

**What it costs them:**
Lost leads, higher CPA, weak trust, slower growth, wasted ad spend, and more owner attention spent managing chaos.

**Emotional tension:**
Frustration, distrust in the data, confusion about what to fix first, and fatigue from managing multiple disconnected providers.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local agencies, freelancers, and performance shops offering websites, ads, or lead generation in Czechia.
**Secondary:** DIY combinations of Wix/Webflow/WordPress builders, ad freelancers, template setups, and in-house experimentation.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing, relying on referrals only, relying on Instagram/Facebook pages without a real site, or staying on outdated websites because replacement feels risky.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- System view across website, ads, tracking, and automation
- Practical, operator-level positioning instead of abstract branding language
- Czech-market relevance with Russian-language communication where needed
- Strong emphasis on lead flow, attribution, and operational follow-through

**How we do it differently:**
We define the page role, the conversion path, the traffic source, and the data layer before treating any deliverable as "done." A site is not shipped as a brochure; ads are not launched into a weak page; automation is not bolted onto a broken process.

**Why that's better:**
The client gets a setup that is easier to trust, measure, and improve over time.

**Why customers choose us:**
Because they want a practical partner who can connect website, traffic, data, and follow-up instead of handing off isolated pieces.

## Objections

| "Мне нужен просто сайт / просто реклама." | A narrower first step is fine, but the work still needs to fit the broader conversion system or it will underperform. |

| "Слишком сложно, я не знаю, с чего начать." | That is exactly why the site needs a clear path: service pages for defined needs, and a diagnostic route when the next step is not obvious yet. |

**Anti-persona:**
Large enterprise procurement processes, clients who want a cheap brochure site with no commercial logic, and projects looking for hype-heavy "creative" positioning without measurable business outcomes.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:**
The current setup wastes money, loses leads, or makes decision-making impossible.

**Pull:**
The promise of a clearer website, better lead flow, cleaner tracking, and one connected growth system.

**Habit:**
Staying with the current site, current ad setup, or current providers because change feels disruptive.

**Anxiety:**
Fear of rebuilding the wrong thing, spending money without clarity, or being pushed into oversized scope.
