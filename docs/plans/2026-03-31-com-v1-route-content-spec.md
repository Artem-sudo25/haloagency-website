## 1. Purpose

This document converts the approved `.com` V1 brief into route-by-route page specs.

Use it when writing copy, designing layouts, or building page components for the Russian-first `.com` version.

Each page spec defines:

- page role
- audience slice
- entry situation
- primary CTA
- section order
- proof allowed on the page
- objections to handle
- wording to use and wording to avoid

## 2. Global Rules

### 2.1 Audience framing

Always frame the audience as:

- Russian-speaking founders, owners, or practical marketing leads
- SMBs operating in Europe
- businesses selling into a local or regional market inside the EU

Do not frame the site as:

- a site for businesses in Russia
- a site that “works equally everywhere in Europe” without local nuance
- a general creative agency for everyone

### 2.2 CTA rule

Every page gets:

- one primary CTA
- one secondary CTA maximum

Across the V1, the safest CTA family is:

- `Обсудить задачу`
- `Получить план`
- `Отправить вводные`

Avoid introducing too many CTA promises such as:

- audit
- strategy session
- growth map
- free demo
- estimate

One page can use one of these if needed, but the overall system should still feel like one path.

### 2.3 Proof rule

Current proof is strong enough for V1, but it is still mostly Czechia-centered.

That means:

- use the current cases as operating proof
- do not present them as proof of native market depth in every EU country
- if needed, add a short note that the current portfolio is concentrated in Czechia and nearby markets

### 2.4 Voice rule

The tone stays:

- direct
- calm
- practical
- commercially focused

Avoid:

- agency hype
- startup jargon
- vague “growth platform” language
- internal IA language like “hub”, “route”, “layer”, “intent”

## 4. Homepage Spec: `/ru/`

### Page role

Position HaloAgency for the new audience, build trust quickly, and route visitors into the right next page.

### Audience slice

- founders and owners who know something is broken, but may not know whether the first fix is site, ads, tracking, or automation

### Entry situations

- referral traffic
- direct traffic
- branded search
- warm traffic from social or outbound

### Primary CTA

`Обсудить задачу`

### Secondary CTA

`Посмотреть кейсы`

### Required sections in order

1. Hero
2. Proof strip
3. Start-here router
4. System explainer
5. Cases preview
6. Fit / Not fit
7. Process
8. Founder / trust
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

### Must communicate

- HaloAgency is a practical partner, not just a supplier of isolated services
- communication and strategy are available in Russian
- execution is localized to the market where the client sells
- the site, ads, tracking, and follow-up must work together

- country-heavy Czech messaging above the fold
- promising every service in full detail
- package pricing blocks
- multiple forms and lead magnets

### Main objections to answer

- “I do not know where to start.”
- “I already have a site or ads.”
- “I do not want to buy more than I need.”
- “Can this work outside Czechia?”

- homepage router
- support page from websites or ads
- follow-up page after audit conversations

`Получить разбор`

### Secondary CTA

`Обсудить задачу`

1. Hero with attribution and lead-flow promise
2. What gets lost without proper tracking and process logic
3. Tracking block
4. Automation block
5. Typical use cases

7. FAQ
8. Final CTA

## 11. Contact Page Spec: `/ru/contact`

### Page role

Capture ready demand with the clearest possible expectation of what the user should send and what happens next.

### Audience slice

- ready-to-talk visitors
- buyers who already understand the offer and want the first step

### Entry situations

- primary CTA from every main page
- direct traffic from outbound or messaging

### Primary CTA

`Отправить вводные`

### Secondary CTA

- direct messenger contact only if maintained reliably

### Required sections in order

1. Hero with expectation-setting
2. What to send
3. Short form
4. What happens after submission
5. Trust note

### Must communicate

- you do not need a perfect brief
- a link to the site, current ads, market, and short task description is enough
- HaloAgency will review and suggest the first reasonable next step
- response time is clear

### Must avoid

- generic “contact us” language
- too many fields
- making the page feel bureaucratic

### Suggested form fields

- name
- company
- website or current landing page
- country or market where the business sells
- what is the main goal right now
- optional current ads or channels
- contact method

### Main objections to answer

- “I do not have everything ready yet.”
- “What if I only need one piece?”
- “Will I be pushed into a large project?”

### Internal links

- none required beyond legal and privacy support

## 13. Writing Guardrails

When writing final copy for any of these routes:

1. Start with the buyer problem, not the service list.
2. Use direct Russian and concrete outcomes.
3. Keep the “system” angle practical, not abstract.
4. Reuse case proof close to the decision point.
5. State clearly when a narrower first step is enough.
6. Do not imply HaloAgency has native local-market depth in every EU country.
7. Do not let the new site drift back into Czech-only wording.
