## Publishing Rules

- Write for Russian-speaking business owners in Czechia.
- Prefer practical diagnostic articles over generic agency thought leadership.
- Each article should link naturally to one primary service page and one supporting page.
- Each article should end with one clear CTA:
  - `Обсудить задачу`
  - or `Отправить короткий бриф`
- Use real business examples, screenshots, checklists, and decision criteria wherever possible.
