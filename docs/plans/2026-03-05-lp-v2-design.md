# /lp/v2 — Free Audit Landing Page Design

**Date:** 2026-03-05
**Route:** `/lp/v2`
**Goal:** High-conversion landing page for a free 20-min diagnostic call + PDF. A/B test against `/lp/experimental`.

---

## The Offer

Free 20-minute call where HaloAgency diagnoses the visitor's specific situation (ads, landing page, tracking, strategy). After the call: a PDF with 3–5 concrete improvements for their case.

Conversion path: Call → PDF demonstrates competence → retainer conversation happens naturally.

The offer is completely free. No paid audit. No price to display.

---

## The Core Trust Problem

Every business owner has been burned by "free audits" that are just sales calls. The page must neutralize this upfront. The word "бесплатно" appears at least 3 times above the fold. The trust statement replaces a guarantee block: *"Это не продажа. После звонка уйдёте с конкретным планом — работаете с нами или нет."*

## Visual Identity

| Token | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#F8F4EF` | Warm off-white, not pure white |
| Card / callout bg | `#F0E8DF` | Warm cream tint |
| Accent | `#C15C32` | Terracotta — not Tailwind emerald |
| Text primary | `#1A1F28` | Dark ink |
| Text muted | `#64707C` | — |
| Result numbers | `#1B5E38` | Forest green, used only for metrics |
| Font | Plus Jakarta Sans | Not Inter |
| Button shape | `rounded-lg` | Not pill, not sharp |
| Button color | Terracotta fill, white text | One shape, one color |

No dark mode. No emerald. No gradient glow. No terminal aesthetic.

## Page Structure (6 sections)

### 1. Sticky Nav
Logo + phone number only. Nothing else.

### 2. Hero (two-column desktop, stacked mobile)
**Left column:**
- H1: "Реклама уходит, а заявок не прибавляется. За 20 минут разберём почему — бесплатно."
- Subhead: what they get (call + PDF), who it's for
- Proof: 2 lines embedded in body text, not stat cards
- Single CTA button: "Записаться на бесплатный разбор"
- Text link below: "или напишите в WhatsApp"

**Right column (form card):**
- Header: "Бесплатный разбор — 20 минут"
- 4 fields: имя, сайт или Instagram, рекламный бюджет, email
- Submit: "Отправить заявку →"
- Below button: "Ответим в WhatsApp за 2 часа в рабочее время"
- No dual CTA inside the form

**Removed from hero:**
- Pill badge above H1
- Stats strip card grid
- Ghost "Получить PDF" secondary button
- Fake "6 слотов" scarcity

### 3. Case Studies (3, full-width stacked)
Each case study card:
- Header: business type + location + budget
- "Ситуация:" — one prose paragraph
- "Что изменили:" — 3 clean bullet points (no `//` or `>` annotations)
- Result metrics displayed large (2xl font, forest green `#1B5E38`)

### 4. Trust + Offer Block (warm `#F0E8DF` background)
- Large statement: "Это не продажа."
- Explanation: after the call you get a concrete PDF plan — whether you work with us or not
- Offer summary: Бесплатный звонок + PDF с приоритетами
- Single CTA button

### 5. FAQ (4 items, `<details>` accordion)
Only the four highest-anxiety questions:
1. Подойдет ли разбор, если трекинг вообще не настроен?
2. Можно взять рекомендации и внедрять самостоятельно?
3. Нужны ли доступы к рекламным кабинетам?
4. Когда виден первый результат после изменений?

### 6. Final CTA Block
H2 + one button + WhatsApp text link. No scarcity. No slots counter.

## CRO Decisions

| Current `/lp/experimental` | `/lp/v2` | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `EXECUTE_BOOKING` | "Отправить заявку →" | Reduces anxiety at submission |
| Dual CTAs everywhere | One button per section | Forces a single decision |
| "4 900 CZK" pricing | Removed entirely | Offer is free |
| Money-back guarantee | Trust statement (не продажа) | Right objection for free offer |
| 9 FAQ items | 4 items | Objection handling only |
| Stats strip (3 cards) | Proof embedded in hero copy | Reads as claims not decoration |
| Deliverables grid (6 cards) | Cut | Case studies do this job better |
| "How it works" steps | Cut | Adds length without adding trust |
| "Why HaloAgency" section | Cut | Case studies + trust block do this |
| `TRUST_SIGNALS` section | Cut | Embedded in nav + form copy |
| Terminal headers (ALL_CAPS) | Plain H2s | Identity mismatch eliminated |
| Dark background + emerald | Warm light + terracotta | Right signal for SMB audience |
| Fake "6 слотов" | Removed | False scarcity destroys credibility |
| Pill badge above H1 | Removed | Let H1 carry its own context |
